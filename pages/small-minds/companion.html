Small Minds · Part 5 · Keystone Project

A Creature of Your Own

Adopt a tiny 3D companion whose brain trains live on your GPU — chat, pet, toss glowing orbs, teach moves, explore five worlds, and put them to sleep to run AutoResearch dreams.

Everything in the sanctuary above runs locally in your browser with zero servers and zero cloud APIs. Click directly on your companion to pet them, double-click the ground (or hit Toss Orb ✦) to play fetch, chat to teach them your name and favorite words, cue and reward physical tricks, or switch between six distinct species and five worlds drawn from the other series on this site. Every weight tensor, episodic memory, trick score, and evolution stage is persisted quietly in your browser's IndexedDB, so when you return tomorrow, your companion picks up right where you left off.

Debug mode · press ;

Hit the semicolon key ; anywhere outside a text box (or add ?debug to the URL) to open the live telemetry overlay: frame rate, frame time, JS heap, active species, 3D model author, WebGPU LLM parameter count, training steps, decode speed, plus one-click buttons to preview evolution stages 1–4.

Architecture · Part 1Continual learning without forgetting

A pet that never changes gets stale in five minutes; a neural network that only trains on the newest message suffers catastrophic forgetting, overwriting its entire vocabulary with the last sentence you typed. Our companion solves this with an episodic replay buffer.

Whenever you send a message, the pet first runs real autoregressive decoding through our pure-WebGPU micro-Transformer (RMSNorm + RoPE + FlashAttention + SwiGLU) and captures its live Layer-0 attention heatmap. It then appends your exchange to its personal memory bank and executes a short burst of conservative WebGPU gradient steps sampled from a mixture of its species personality seed, core dialogue, and your personal memories. Open the Brain Peek tab above after a few chats to inspect the live attention map, falling cross-entropy loss, and raw token samples straight from GPU memory.

Architecture · Part 2Teaching moves with policy gradients

Chat is only one channel. Real companions learn physical habits through play and reinforcement. Inside the Teach Moves tab, six procedural animations (Hop, Spin, Sing, Zoomies, Bow, and Starflip) sit behind a compact policy weight table.

When you cue a move and click Reward ✦, a reward-modulated policy step raises that trick's logit and mastery score while boosting bond and experience. As mastery climbs, your companion begins performing favorite learned moves spontaneously when happy and unlocks higher visual evolution stages.

Architecture · Part 3Recursive self-improvement while dreaming

Why should a pet stop learning when the conversation pauses? Tap Start Dream Cycle ☽ in the Dream tab and the sanctuary dims to twilight. Your creature closes its eyes and breathes slowly while the Part 4 Browser AutoResearcher wakes up in the background, running UCB-bandit optimization experiments over your pet's personal replay buffer on WebGPU and consolidating calmer, lower-loss weights before waking back up.

Architecture · Part 4Five worlds from across this site

Every sanctuary backdrop borrows an idea from an earlier series in Algorythmic Explorations:

  • Meadow Valley (Ground Truth) — procedural terrain contour, hundreds of instanced wind-swaying grass blades, and drifting twilight fireflies.
  • Starlight Nebula (Particle Worlds) — a floating crystal plinth ringed by Keplerian orbital dust motes that accelerate when your pet plays.
  • Bonfire Clearing (Bounced Light) — a flickering warm campfire sending luminous embers curling into the dark sky.
  • Harmonic Hall (Living Music & Acoustic Spaces) — concentric acoustic floor rings that pulse with gentle pentatonic Web Audio chimes whenever your pet hops or sings.
  • Ferro Sanctuary (Strange Matter) — a glossy obsidian magnetic pool that ripples and raises Rosensweig-style spikes whenever your pet moves or you tap the floor.

Architecture · Part 5Six evolving species, built to grow to twenty

Each species is defined by a self-contained procedural 3D rig implementing a shared SpeciesDefinition interface — smooth look-at eye tracking, natural blinks, happy crescent squints when petted, neural glow tied to live WebGPU attention energy, and four visual evolution stages (Hatchling → Curious Sprout → Radiant Friend → Starlight Sage) that unlock collars, constellations, spirit fox tails, and celestial crowns.

Adding a seventh or twentieth creature only takes registering one more builder in speciesRegistry.ts. Spin the gallery turntable below to inspect all six companions across every evolution stage and watch their signature moves up close.

From a single 16×16 shared-memory tile in Part 1 to a tiny evolving creature dreaming better weights in your browser tab — every layer of the stack is right here in the source, open to poke, break, and expand with new species, new worlds, and new learning rules.
